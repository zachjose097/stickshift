Wrap up this session. Use the stickshift skill.

1. Add today's entry to `.stickshift/journal.md`: what they did, what was hard, unlocks (with
   paths; read today's from `.stickshift/.hook/unlocks.log`, without writing to it), and what's next.
2. Make sure `decisions.md`, `concepts.md`, `map.md` and `questions.md` reflect the session.
3. Show a short recap in chat:
   - What they built and what they learned (two or three lines).
   - Decisions made.
   - Concepts still `unconfirmed` from today. Offer teach-backs for up to two of them, one at a
     time, all optional.
   - Open questions.
   - Suggested next step.
4. End with one or two lines of progress over the last week, from the notes: known concepts in the
   profile (count then and now, by the dates), concepts confirmed, quiz score, and a concept whose
   `Hints` rung went down ("Loops: rung 3 → rung 1"). Only show numbers that changed; skip the
   section in a project's first week if there's nothing to compare.
